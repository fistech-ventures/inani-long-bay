Discover Inani Long Bay

Nestled along the pristine shores of Inani Beach, Hotel Inani Long Bay offers an unparalleled beachfront experience in Cox's Bazar. Our luxury resort combines modern elegance with the natural beauty of Bangladesh's longest beach.

With our commitment to exceptional service and warm hospitality, we ensure every guest experiences the perfect blend of relaxation and adventure. From our stunning ocean views to our world-class amenities, your stay with us will be nothing short of extraordinary.
